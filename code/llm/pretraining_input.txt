Every morning, Mira walked to the little library beside the river. She opened
the windows, arranged the books, and wrote a new question on the chalkboard.
Today she wrote: What can we learn by looking closely?

A red bird landed outside. Mira noticed its quick feet and bright feathers. A
cloud crossed the sun, and the bird's shadow disappeared. She recorded each
small observation in a blue notebook. By noon, the pages held a simple lesson:
careful questions lead to new ideas, and new ideas lead to better questions.

The next morning, Mira returned to the library and began again.
